# Minutes of Meeting Generator

## What This Does

Transforms meeting transcripts, notes, or recordings into professional, copy-ready Minutes of the Meeting following a standard institutional format.

## When to Use This

Use this skill whenever you need to:
- Document official meetings
- Create formal meeting records for internal divisions
- Generate minutes for partner/stakeholder meetings
- Produce institutional-grade meeting documentation

## How to Use

Simply say one of these trigger phrases:
- **"Generate meeting minutes"**
- **"Create minutes of meeting"**
- **"Format meeting minutes"**
- **"Write MOM"**

Then provide:
1. **Meeting details** (title, date, time, venue, division, reference number)
2. **Attendees list** (grouped by organization with names and roles)
3. **Meeting content** (transcript, notes, or summary)
4. **Action items** (if any, with owners and timelines)

## What You'll Get

A complete minutes document with:
- Standardized header metadata
- Six Roman numeral sections (I–VI):
  - I. Call to Order
  - II. Approval of Provisional Agenda
  - III. Review of Minutes
  - IV. Updates on Decision-Action Items
  - V. Discussions (structured bullets)
  - VI. Adjournment
- Objective, factual language
- Clear action items with owners
- Proper sign-offs (Prepared By / Reviewed By)

## Example Usage

**You:** "Generate minutes for our partnership meeting on Feb 5, 2026"

**Agent:** "I'll create the minutes. Please provide:
- Meeting title and division
- Start/end time and venue
- Attendees (grouped by organization)
- Meeting notes or transcript
- Any action items discussed"

## Output Format

The skill produces a document ready to paste into your official template, following institutional standards with proper formatting, tone, and structure.

## Quality Standards

- **Objective third-person** past tense
- **No speculation** - only documented facts
- **MECE discussion clusters** - no duplicated points
- **Clear action items** with explicit owners
- **Professional tone** suitable for institutional records
